You've been phished!

This was a simulated phishing message sent as part of our security awareness program. You interacted with a message designed to look legitimate—but this is a safe opportunity to learn.

No blame—just awareness.

Phishing messages can be convincing. Taking a moment to check before clicking can help protect you and the organization.

Spot the signs next time

1

Check the sender. Look carefully at the full email address and watch for unfamiliar or misspelled domains.

2

Pause before clicking. Be cautious of urgent requests, unexpected attachments, and links asking you to sign in.

3

Report suspicious messages. Use your organization's approved phishing-reporting process or contact IT/security.

If you entered a password or other sensitive information into a page you now suspect is unsafe, contact your IT/security team promptly and follow their instructions.

Think before you click. When in doubt, verify through a trusted channel.
Questions? Contact your IT or security team.
